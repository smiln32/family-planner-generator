# Design system

Canonical print rules for the Family Success Planner. Templates and `familyplanner/static/planner.css` implement these. Change a rule here first, then the CSS or templates.

## Page box

- Three paper sizes: `5.5x8` (5.5 by 8 in), `6x9` (6 by 9 in), and `letter` (8.5 by 11 in). Default is `letter`.
- One printed margin for every sheet, set on the page box in `templates/book.html` (`--page-pad-*`). Dense pages tighten interior spacing. They never shrink the outer margin.
- Print at 100% / Actual size. Do not fit-to-page. Use paper that matches `page_size`.
- The worksheets are laid out in inches for letter. On `5.5x8` and `6x9` a worksheet is laid out at letter width and scaled to the page (CSS `zoom` by `render.page_zoom`: the small page's text width over letter's, about 0.62 and 0.68), with its margins kept at their printed size; it gets proportionally more height, which its flexible rows take. The covers, the look and layout month pages and the layout sheets size themselves (`--k`, `--u`; `--u` also keeps to the page height) and are not scaled; the budget page keeps its own small-page fit. A zoomed worksheet uses the letter layout unit (`--u: 0.024in`). The annual calendar's dates print at 5.5pt or more and the health tracker's column notes are left out on the small sizes. Every card, table and tab sits inside the margins at every size (`tests/test_page_fit.py`).

## Type

Three roles, filled by a named typeface set in config or a custom `display` / `script` / `body` trio. There are 52 sets, defined in `familyplanner/typefaces.py` and listed by `python planner.py typefaces`: six general sets (`elegant`, `classic`, `playful`, `modern`, `italics`, `neat`), two pairings for each retired look (for example `boho-brush`, once called `terracotta`; the old name still works), and one set for each of the thirteen looks (Swiss grid uses `grotesk-red`).

One rule for lettering: a look or layout sets its own typeface set, and a `fonts:` entry in the config (or a set picked in the editor) overrides it. The default book is the `editorial` look in `home-edition` (Bodoni Moda, Archivo). Blank and the retired looks have no set of their own, so they use `elegant` (Playfair Display, Great Vibes, Lato) unless `fonts:` names another.

- Display: chunky headings
- Script: handwritten greeting lines
- Body: worksheets, tables, footers
- Masthead titles (the worksheet title at the top, such as "Household Chores") are one line in one colour, in every design: the design's main title colour and style (`page_title()` in `templates/macros.html`). They use the display size (`type_size.title`, default 30pt) and shrink only as far as a long title must to fit its masthead at the page size (no script; see `.pt-fit` in `planner.css`). The subtitle, banner or year under a title stays its own line. They sit at the top of the header row, not vertically centred on the house art. Cover stack uses `type_size.cover`. Banners stay their own role; sliders in the visual editor change the four `type_size` values. Carla approved one-line, one-colour titles on 2026-10-11.
- The book has thirteen looks (`designs.LOOKS`): `editorial` (default), `midcentury`, `nordic`, `farmhouse`, `deco`, `swiss`, `retro`, `bauhaus`, `almanac`, `terracotta`, `cottage`, `gazette`, `coastal`. Each brings its own cover (`templates/look_cover.html`), monthly page (`templates/look_monthly.html`), worksheet chrome and colour palette (`static/looks.css`) and typeface set. Approved by the owner on 2026-09-30 from the design canvas `Planner Styles.dc.html`.
- Covers are type, colour and geometry only, with no illustration. As designed they print no family name and no year. Three opt-in settings, all off by default, add words in one small spot per look (`.lko-<look>` in `static/looks.css`), set in that look's own fonts and colours and placed in its open space without moving anything else: `cover_show_family` ("The [name] Family"), `cover_show_year` (`off`, `year`, or `custom` for the `cover_text` line) and `cover_line` (one extra line, 80 characters). Long lines step down in size (`cfg.cover_fit`: `.lko-fit2`, `.lko-fit3`), and a word too long for the spot breaks inside it. With all three off, a look cover is pixel-identical to the approved design. Cover and monthly sizes are in `--k` (1/408 of the page width). A cover is drawn for its look's own typeface set; when another set is picked, each cover title line keeps to its room and scales down to fit (`fit()` in `look_cover.html`, `.cf` in `looks.css`), and in the own set the cover prints exactly as designed.
- Icons in round badges (`chip()`) are centred by their drawing: each icon symbol in `templates/icons.html` has its `viewBox` set so its ink sits in the middle of its 24x24 box. A new badge icon must be centred the same way (`tests/test_icon_centring.py` checks it).
- Colours are three layers (`config.resolve_palette`, the one rule for `build`, the editor and plan files): the design's own palette (Blank and the retired looks start from the `linen` factory palette), then a named `theme` over it, then the config's `palette:` block. A theme recolours any design, and tokens it does not name stay the design's own. No theme (`theme: ""`, the default) prints the design as designed. The editor shows the theme cards for every design, leading with "Design's own colours", and its pickers start from the layered base. Lettering follows the one rule under Type above and is resolved separately from colour.
- Look monthly pages close with their own write-on blocks, not the footer macro.
- Retired looks (`original`, `02`–`17`) still resolve so old plan files build, but are not offered in the picker.
- Planner layouts `02a` to `12c` cross one of looks `02`, `06`, `11`, `12` with a page structure (`a` split, `b` stacked bands, `c` sidebar). The body carries both classes (`design-06 design-06b`), so the look's paper and chrome still apply; the layout adds its own box style (`box-06b`) to its own cover (title, year and the twelve months at a glance), a boxed monthly page and the `weekly` and `daily` sheets. A layout book is 32 pages by default. Each layout family has its own typeface set: `02` ledger, `06` composition-book, `11` boardroom, `12` garden-party. Parts live in `templates/layout_kit.html`, `templates/layout_monthly.html`, `templates/pages/daily.html`, `templates/pages/weekly.html` and the "planner layouts" section at the end of `planner.css`. Sizes there are in `--u` (1/300 of the page width) so a sheet keeps its proportions at every page size. Added 2026-09-30 from the owner's design export.
- `blank` hides illustration.

Colour is twelve tokens on the palette (`navy`, `teal`, `pink`, `orange`, `green`, `purple`, `blue`, `red`, `brown`, `ink`, `paper`, `rule`). The `linen` factory palette (what Blank and the retired looks print in without a theme) is warm beige and ivory. `classic` is the original bright printable. `adventure` (forest green, saddle brown, brick red on cream) and `goal` (ink navy, old gold, rust on white) name only `navy`, `teal`, `red` and `paper`, so the design beneath shows through in the other eight. Each named theme should read as its name at a glance (`citrus` stays lemon/lime/tangerine, `ocean` stays blue/green/seafoam with no coral or red, `forest` stays green/brown, `poppy` stays pink/red with no cyan). When adding or editing a theme, keep its twelve tokens inside that colour family rather than defaulting to a generic dark-navy-plus-bright-accent scheme. A config `palette:` block overrides individual tokens.

### Four axes (Phase 1, 2026-10-12)

Pages, chrome, colours and type resolve independently through the existing keys: `pages` (checked at plan time in `render.plan_pages`, against the templates that exist), `design`, `theme` plus `palette`, and `fonts`. Config checks them in the order design, theme, palette, fonts; checking is separate from resolving. Phase 1 left one limitation, that `planner.css` fixed the layouts' chrome colours on their body classes; resolved in Phase 2 step 1 (commit `bf3c78c` on `chrome-catalog`, 2026-10-12): the layouts `02a` to `12c` read the palette tokens and the chrome roles below from `:root`, `tests/test_css_tokens.py` guards it, and only the retired looks' classes (`.design-03`, `-04`, `-05`, `-09`, `-13` to `-17`) still carry their own token blocks.

## Colour roles

Carla approved this rule on 2026-10-12. It holds on every page in every design (the thirteen looks, the twelve layouts, blank and original); covers, page titles, fonts, textures and art keep their own colours. `familyplanner/colours.py` picks the colours from the design's palette; templates read them as `p.structure`, `p.weekend`, `p.winter` ...; CSS reads `--c-structure`, `--c-weekend`, `--c-winter` ... and `--c-<role>-on`, the text colour that reads on each (white or the palette's ink, at least 4.5:1).

1. **Structure**: one colour per design, its title colour (the palette's `navy`). Every table header, card tab or heading, section label, column header, banner and badge or numbered circle uses it. Nothing alternates.
2. **Weekend**: one accent per design (its `red`, darkened until it reads 5.3:1 on paper, so 4.5:1 on light cells; Almanac uses its ochre, Boxed records a grey, Executive desk a muted brick, decided 2026-10-12 pending Carla's look). Saturday and Sunday labels, day pills, weekend columns and weekend dates use it, everywhere (worksheets, month pages, weekly and daily sheets, the annual calendar). Weekdays use the structure colour. The accent is used for nothing else on a worksheet, not even totals (totals sit on a light wash of the structure colour). Farmhouse sets its titles in the same barn red; titles keep their colour.
3. **Seasons**: four colours per design from its own palette: winter (December, January, February), spring (March to May), summer (June to August), autumn (September to November). They colour the annual calendar's month boxes, the month entries in the contents and tab rail, and the month accents of the pages that show one (the month number on Editorial and Swiss grid, the script line and art on Blank and original). The looks, layouts and themes have hand-picked seasons (`colours.PICKS`), at least delta E 15 apart; with a theme named, the theme's picks are used on any design (`render.colour_roles`), so `swiss` and the `02` layouts get season colours when recoloured; where a palette has too few colours (School notebook, Executive desk, Deco, Gazette, Coastal) they are tints or shades of its own colours. Swiss grid and Boxed records have no colours to spare: their months stay in the structure colour and the season icons carry the season (decided 2026-10-12, pending Carla's look). A month's `accent` in the config is `season` by default; a palette name still gives that month its own colour in Blank and the retired looks.
4. **Neutral fills** (row stripes, cell backgrounds, paper, light washes) stay neutral, and a fill is never a colour from outside the palette. The budget donut keeps one colour per category, because a chart needs them, starting with the structure colour.

Chrome roles (Phase 2 step 1, 2026-10-12). Carla's rule: "when a theme is selected it must also control the colours used for frames, marks, borders and decorative accents. Chrome controls their shape and placement, not fixed colours." The layout chrome in `planner.css` therefore reads five more roles (`--c-frame`, `--c-mark` with `--c-mark-on`, `--c-title`, `--c-trim`, `--c-line`; `p.frame` ... in templates) and never a fixed colour:

5. **Frame**: box and card borders, the layout cover's year and mini-calendar letters. School notebook its pale blue rule, Beautiful day its lilac; otherwise the structure colour.
6. **Mark**: a sheet's accent: School notebook's red margin line, Beautiful day's sage dashes and badges, every other word of a tinted cover title; otherwise the structure colour. `mark-on` is the text colour that reads on it.
7. **Title**: a design's display titles (Beautiful day's rose script); otherwise the structure colour.
8. **Trim**: a second border (Beautiful day's sage page border); otherwise the palette's `rule`.
9. **Line**: calendar grid and write-on lines (Executive desk's darker grid); otherwise the palette's `rule`.

Precedence: with a theme named, every role, chrome roles included, comes from the theme's picks and these defaults on the merged palette (design palette, then theme, then the `palette:` block), so the frames, marks and titles of any layout take the theme's structure colour and its lines the theme's rule; the design family's own picks (`colours.PICKS`) are used only when no theme is named. A pick that is a token name follows a `palette:` override; a hex pick is the design's default only. The chrome roles print as picked (frames and lines are borders; marks and titles are display colours that keep their own colour, like page titles), so with no theme every layout prints exactly as drawn.

Shared worksheet chrome (Phase 2 step 2, 2026-10-12, branch `chrome-catalog`; commit hash in `HANDOFF.md`). The chrome every design shares in `planner.css` no longer prints fixed colours: the meta strips at the top of a worksheet (family time, school, emergency, vacation, home maintenance, the month focus strip) and the "belongs to" card border read `--c-strip`, which is the frame role (`--c-frame`; derived on `:root` like `--c-wash`); every table and calendar hairline, write-on line (`.dot-line`, `.solid-line`), dotted divider and the index's dotted rows read `--c-line`; check boxes read `--c-frame`; the day pill on a month grid is a 4% wash of the structure colour in white (the `--c-wash` recipe; 4% is the strongest wash on which every design's and theme's weekday and weekend numbers still read at 4.5:1); the two muted labels (`.emg-name em`, `.index-lede`) print in the structure colour. So a theme recolours the strips, grids and lines of any design, the black-and-white layouts print no stray blue, and every design's output changed slightly (samples in the session scratch `step2/compare/`, awaiting Carla's review). The strip border is the full frame colour, not a wash: on Farmhouse, Mid-century and Boxed records the cards under the strip already draw a full-strength frame, so a wash read as a faded copy of it.

Exceptions that are a look's art, not chrome: the Bauhaus month pages keep their red, blue and butter shapes and coloured Plan/Make/Do blocks; Beautiful day's pink card frames and page border are its palette `pink` (a theme that names `pink` moves them); the Farmhouse stripe, Cottage gingham, Coastal stripe and the Mid-century and Bauhaus corner shapes keep their colours.

`tests/test_colour_roles.py` checks every page of every design in Chrome. `tests/test_css_tokens.py` checks that `planner.css` and `looks.css` declare no palette token or `--c-*` role outside `:root` and that no rule outside `:root` prints a fixed colour (the retired looks, their title-page parts, the three look-art colours and the budget donut rule excepted, each with its reason in the test). `tests/test_four_axis.py` checks that text on the day pill wash and the muted labels reads at 4.5:1 for every design with and without each theme.

## Variety between designs

Carla said on 2026-10-12 that pages may differ from one design to the next. A page type (Family Values, Family Goals, Budget and so on) must cover the same basic information in every design, but its layout, extra sections and style can change from design to design; a section on one design's page need not appear on the same page in another. Variety is welcome. What stays fixed everywhere is the colour roles above and that everything fits its page.

## Cards and chrome

- `.card-tab` is 8pt everywhere. A label longer than its card wraps onto a second line inside the card's width; the first line stays where a one-line tab sits and the extra line pushes the card's content down (see "Everything fits its page" in `looks.css`). Prefer a label that fits on one line: in a layout book a worksheet tab is about 8pt at letter (`4.6 * --u`; the script layouts about 10pt), and a label that wraps at letter should be shortened.
- Every page ends with the shared footer macro, except cover, belongs_to, how_to_use, and values, which have their own bottom art.
- Illustrations are inline SVG in `templates/art.html` and the icon sprite in `templates/icons.html`. No network images at build time.

## Months

Twelve monthly sheets share `monthly.html`. Dated grids use `year` and `week_starts`. `dated_months: false` prints blank grids.

- Blank and the retired looks print each month's accent, motif, tagline and footer line from config.
- The thirteen looks use `look_monthly.html` and the layouts use `layout_monthly.html`. Both print the month name with the look's own wording and write-on blocks; they ignore the config's taglines, motifs, accents and footer lines.

Available motifs: snowman, hearts, sprout, tulips, flowers, sun, beach, school, pumpkin, leaves, snowflake.

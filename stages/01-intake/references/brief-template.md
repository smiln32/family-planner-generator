# Run brief template

Copy this shape into `output/[slug]-brief.md`. Replace the bracketed values.

```markdown
# [slug] brief

- year: [2027]
- family_name: [Ramirez or empty]
- week_starts: [sunday|monday]
- dated_months: [true|false]
- page_size: [letter|a4]
- members:
  - [name or empty line]
```

Notes for customize go under a "Notes" heading: pages to drop, gift vs household use, anything the family asked for. Do not put hex colours here.
